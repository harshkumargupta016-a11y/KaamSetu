Help & Q/A

Is KaamSetu free for job seekers?

Yes. Searching, applying and the skill-gap report are free for workers.

How is the Fit Score calculated?

40% distance, 30% experience match, 30% company trust score.

Can I apply without internet?

Yes, via SMS code or by saving a walk-in slot that syncs later.

How do employers get a Verified badge?

By verifying Udyam or MCA registration and a business phone number.

Is my data safe?

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act principles: consent, minimal data, and deletion on request.

I was asked to pay a fee for a job. What now?

Stop, do not pay, use the Complain form, and call 1930 or the Ministry of Labour helpline 14434.
