Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What is KaamSetu and how does it work?

KaamSetu is an AI-powered employer discovery and hiring platform. It matches job seekers to nearby vacancies with a transparent Fit Score, verifies employer trust via Gemini AI & Admin checks, and operates seamlessly even in low-bandwidth regions.

Q: Is KaamSetu free for job seekers?

Yes! Searching for jobs, 1-click applying, voice navigation, and using the AI Profile Scanner & Skill-Gap report are 100% free for all job seekers.

Q: How is the Gemini Fit Score calculated?

The Gemini Fit Score combines 40% proximity distance, 30% skill & experience overlap, and 30% company trust rating to ensure workers get matched to safe, nearby jobs.

Q: How does KaamSetu verify new companies & block scams?

When a company registers or posts a job, Gemini AI evaluates Udyam/MCA credentials, wage levels, and fraud report history to assign a Trust Score. Admin control officers conduct final manual verification, badging verified employers with ☑️.

Q: Can I use KaamSetu without internet or with weak signal?

Yes. KaamSetu is built with PWA Service Worker offline caching. You can view saved jobs offline, book walk-in interview slots, or submit applications via offline SMS sync when your connection returns.

Q: How do I report a fake job offer or fraudulent company?

Go to the 🚨 Complain page in the main navigation. You can upload fraud screenshots or capture camera evidence. Gemini AI will run instant fraud confidence analysis and alert the Admin portal for immediate action & helpline 1930 reporting.

Q: Is my personal data and location tracked continuously?

NO. KaamSetu strictly respects the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) principles. Your location is accessed only ONCE when you click "Detect My Location" on the jobs map. We do not store GPS movement trails or share private data.
